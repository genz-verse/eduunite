Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how EduUnite collects, uses and protects information submitted through our website and counselling services.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an enquiry or interact with our website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide.

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Academic information
  • Preferred Online MBA specialisation
  • Programme and university preferences
  • Information related to your enquiry or counselling requirements

2. Automatically Collected Information

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including browser information, referring pages, device information and website interaction data.

We may also collect campaign or advertising parameters such as UTM parameters, Google Click IDs and similar attribution information when applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted to EduUnite may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry.
  • Understand your academic profile and preferences.
  • Provide personalised Online MBA counselling and university shortlisting.
  • Assist with programme comparison and the admission process.
  • Contact you regarding relevant educational opportunities or services.
  • Improve our website, services and user experience.

4. Communication

By submitting your contact details, you agree that EduUnite representatives may contact you regarding your enquiry through phone calls, email, SMS, WhatsApp or other appropriate communication channels.

You may request that we stop promotional communications by contacting us.

5. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and analytics technologies to understand website usage, measure advertising performance and improve our services.

These technologies may include services such as Google Analytics and Meta Pixel.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorised service providers, counselling teams or relevant educational institutions when necessary to assist with your enquiry, application or admission requirements.

7. Data Security

EduUnite takes reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information from unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, universities or external services. EduUnite is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

9. Your Choices

You may contact us if you wish to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable requirements.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will be published on this page with the revised update date.
