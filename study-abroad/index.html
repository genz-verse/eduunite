Frequently Asked Questions

Questions Students Ask Before Studying Business & Technology in Europe

Can I study Business, Computer Science, or AI in Europe after Class 12?+
Yes. Students who have completed Class 12 from a recognised board can apply for undergraduate programmes in Business Administration, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Software Engineering, and other technology-related fields, subject to university eligibility requirements.
Which European countries are best for Business and Technology courses?+
Countries such as Germany, France, Georgia, Ireland, Hungary, Poland, Lithuania, Malta, Cyprus, and the Netherlands offer high-quality education in Business, Computer Science, AI, Data Science, and Information Technology with globally recognised degrees.
Do I need IELTS to study in Europe?+
IELTS requirements vary by university and country. Many European universities accept alternative English proficiency tests or may waive IELTS if your previous education was in English.
What are the tuition fees for Business and Technology courses in Europe?+
Tuition fees depend on the university, country, and programme. Students should also consider accommodation, food, health insurance, visa fees, and living expenses while planning their budget.
Are scholarships available for international students?+
Yes. Many European universities offer merit-based scholarships, tuition fee discounts, and financial aid for eligible international students. Eligibility varies by institution.
Can I work part-time while studying in Europe?+
Most European countries allow international students to work part-time during their studies, subject to local immigration regulations and visa conditions.
What career opportunities are available after completing these courses?+
Graduates can pursue careers in Software Development, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Cloud Computing, Business Analytics, Digital Marketing, Finance, Consulting, Product Management, and Entrepreneurship.
How can EduUnite help with admissions and visas?+
EduUnite assists students with university selection, application processing, documentation, scholarships, visa guidance, accommodation support, and pre-departure assistance. Final admission and visa decisions are made by the respective universities and immigration authorities.
