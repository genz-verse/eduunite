Your Information Matters

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EduUnite collects, uses, stores and protects personal information submitted through this website.

Effective date: 4 August 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the EduUnite website, enquiry forms, counselling requests and related communications. By submitting information through the website, you acknowledge the practices described below.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect:

  • Your name, mobile or WhatsApp number and email address.
  • Your course interest, Class 12 marks or percentage, preferred country and intended intake.
  • Information you voluntarily provide during counselling or follow-up communication.
  • Page URL, referring page, form submission time and campaign parameters such as UTM values, GCLID, GBRAID or WBRAID where available.
  • Basic technical or usage information processed by our hosting, analytics, advertising or form-storage providers.

3. How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Review your profile and prepare suitable university or programme options.
  • Contact you by phone, WhatsApp, email or another method you have provided.
  • Provide counselling, application guidance and requested information.
  • Improve the website, measure campaign performance and prevent misuse or spam.
  • Maintain records, respond to requests and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Consent and communication

When you submit an enquiry, you authorise EduUnite and its authorised counsellors to contact you regarding study options and related services. You may withdraw consent or ask us to stop promotional communication by contacting us using the details below.

5. How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with authorised counsellors, technology and storage providers, analytics or advertising providers, and universities or application partners when this is necessary to provide a service you requested or when you ask us to proceed with an application.

These recipients may operate in India or another country. We limit sharing to information reasonably required for the relevant purpose.

6. Google services and third-party tools

Website forms may use Google Apps Script, Google Sheets or comparable services for submission and storage. The website may also use analytics, advertising measurement, communication or hosting tools. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

7. Storage and retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide counselling, manage your enquiry, maintain legitimate business records, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Information may be deleted, anonymised or archived when it is no longer required.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information. No website, internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request:

  • Access to information associated with your enquiry.
  • Correction, completion or updating of inaccurate information.
  • Deletion of information where retention is not legally or operationally required.
  • Withdrawal of consent or discontinuation of promotional contact.
  • Resolution of a privacy-related complaint or grievance.

10. Students under 18

Students below the age of 18 should use this website with the involvement of a parent or lawful guardian. Where required, we may request parental or guardian confirmation before processing an application or providing certain services.

11. External links

The website may link to universities, service providers or other third-party websites. EduUnite is not responsible for the content, security or privacy practices of external websites.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The revised effective date will be shown at the top of this page.

13. Contact and grievance requests

For privacy questions, consent withdrawal, correction, deletion or grievance requests, email admissions@eduunite.com. You may also write to EduUnite, Noida, Delhi NCR, India.
