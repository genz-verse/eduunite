Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EduUnite collects and uses information submitted through this BBA Abroad landing page.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email, state, academic status, preferred destination, preferred intake, enquiry source and advertising attribution parameters such as UTM values or click identifiers.

How we use information

  • To respond to counselling and admission enquiries.
  • To evaluate programme and university suitability.
  • To provide application, document and visa-file guidance.
  • To measure and improve campaign and website performance.
  • To comply with legal and operational requirements.

Sharing

Information may be shared with authorised EduUnite team members, service providers and selected educational institutions when needed for an application and after appropriate student or parent discussion. We do not sell personal data.

Retention and security

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably required for counselling, application support, legal compliance and service improvement. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet transmission is completely risk-free.

Your choices

You may request correction, deletion or withdrawal from promotional communication by contacting us. Some records may be retained where legally or operationally required.
