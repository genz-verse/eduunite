Simple workflowHow to Use the MAT College Predictor for MBA / PGDM Admissions

Selecting the right B-School based on your AIMA MAT percentile requires balancing ROI, accreditation, specialization, and cutoff trends. The MAT College Predictor matches your score against multi-year cutoff data to help you target the best institutions.

Understanding MAT Cutoff Percentiles & Shortlisting

B-Schools evaluate candidates based on MAT percentiles for GD/PI rounds: • Top Govt Universities (95-99.5%ile): PUMBA Pune and SIMSREE Mumbai offer premier education at extremely low fees (~₹1.35L total). • Premier PGDM Institutes (75-85%ile): BIMTECH Greater Noida, XIME Bangalore, and Jaipuria Institute of Management offer strong corporate placements (9-11 LPA avg).

Key Stages in MAT B-School Admission

  • AIMA MAT Scorecard: AIMA conducts MAT four times a year (February, May, September, December) in PBT, CBT, and IBT modes. Top percentiles are valid for 1 academic year.
  • Individual B-School Applications: Applicants must apply separately to participating B-Schools (e.g. BIMTECH, XIME, Jaipuria, JIMS) using their MAT registration number.
  • GD-PI & WAT Rounds: Shortlisted candidates undergo Group Discussion, Written Ability Test, and Personal Interview rounds for final seat conversion.

Decision supportMAT Cutoffs & MBA Fee Structure Overview

Understanding AIMA MAT percentile requirements, fee slabs, ROI calculations, and seat allocation rules across top B-Schools.

MAT Percentile & Fee Structure Categories

MAT accepting institutions are broadly classified into 3 tuition fee and ROI categories:

  • Government Universities (PUMBA / SIMSREE): ₹1.35 Lakhs to ₹1.40 Lakhs total tuition fee for 2 years (Highest ROI in India). Cutoff: 95-99.5%ile.
  • Premier Autonomous B-Schools (BIMTECH / XIME / Jaipuria): ₹9.0 Lakhs to ₹14.0 Lakhs total tuition fee. Cutoff: 75-85%ile. Average Placements: ₹9 to ₹11.5 LPA.
  • Regional Tier-2 Institutes (JIMS / IPE / NDIM / ITM): ₹7.5 Lakhs to ₹10.9 Lakhs total tuition fee. Cutoff: 65-75%ile. Average Placements: ₹7.5 to ₹8.7 LPA.
  • Private Universities (UPES / Amity / Alliance / SRM): ₹7.5 Lakhs to ₹16.2 Lakhs total tuition fee. Cutoff: 60-70%ile.

Selection Rounds & MAT Score Validity

Key guidelines for AIMA MAT score evaluation:

  • Multiple Session Attempts: Candidates can take MAT in February, May, September, or December. Colleges consider the highest percentile achieved.
  • Weightage Criteria: MAT Percentile (45-50%), GD/PI/WAT (30-35%), Academic Background 10th/12th/Graduation (10-15%), Work Ex (5-10%).
  • AICTE & UGC Compliance: All PGDM institutions listed are AICTE approved and equivalent to MBA degrees via AIU accreditation.

Need clarity?Frequently Asked Questions

What is the MAT College Predictor and how does it work?
The MAT College Predictor is a free online admission tool that estimates your MBA and PGDM seat allotment chances across 600+ premier MAT accepting B-Schools (such as PUMBA, SIMSREE, BIMTECH, XIME, Jaipuria, JIMS, IPE, KSOM) based on official AIMA MAT percentile cutoffs. Enter your percentile score to view High, Medium, or Low probabilities.
What MAT percentile is required for top MBA colleges?
Flagship government institutes like PUMBA Pune (~95%ile) and SIMSREE Mumbai (~99.5%ile) require high MAT percentiles for All India seats. Top private B-Schools like BIMTECH Greater Noida (~82%ile), XIME Bangalore (~75-80%ile), and Jaipuria Institute (~75%ile) accept 75+ percentiles for PGDM programs.
Is MAT score valid for both MBA and PGDM programs?
Yes, AIMA MAT scores are recognized by over 600+ B-Schools and UGC universities across India for both 2-year full-time AICTE-approved PGDM programs and University MBA degrees.
How is MAT Percentile calculated vs Composite Score?
MAT Composite Score ranges from 199 to 801 based on performance in 4 core sections (Language Comprehension, Mathematical Skills, Data Analysis & Sufficiency, Intelligence & Critical Reasoning). MAT Percentile indicates your relative rank among all test takers in that exam session.
Do B-Schools conduct GD/PI rounds after MAT shortlisting?
Yes, after obtaining your MAT scorecard, shortlisted candidates are called for Group Discussion (GD), Micro Presentation / Written Ability Test (WAT), and Personal Interview (PI) rounds for final admission selection.
